Explain Newton's first law in simple terms and give me one example.
Newton’s First Law An object will stay still or keep moving in a straight line at the same speed unless a force makes it change. This is also called the law of inertia. Inertia means that objects resist changes to their motion. Example: A book on a table stays still until someone pushes it. The push is a force that changes its motion.
