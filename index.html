⚠️ Important Examination Rules

  • Each question is displayed for exactly 5 seconds.
  • If you do not answer before the time expires, the question is recorded as an empty answer.
  • After you open an examination question page, your examination access for today is permanently used.
  • If you leave the Examination Room, go back, or open another area of AGULIBRARY, you cannot reopen an examination question page until tomorrow.
  • Your examination result and certificate record are stored permanently after submission.
